The sourcing problem

Sourcing from India should create opportunities, not uncertainty.

India offers access to thousands of manufacturers, competitive production costs, skilled craftsmanship, and a wide range of product categories.

But finding a supplier online is easy. Finding the right supplier is not. When you are operating from another country, one wrong supplier decision can cost months of time and thousands of dollars.

Suppliers who look credible online but cannot meet production requirements
Inconsistent quotations, specifications, and minimum order quantities
Poor communication across time zones
Samples that do not match final production
Missed deadlines and unexpected delays
Quality problems discovered after the order has shipped
Limited visibility into what is happening inside the factory

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does a sourcing agent do?

A sourcing agent represents a buyer in a manufacturing market. We help identify suppliers, verify capabilities, collect quotations, coordinate samples, negotiate terms, monitor production, and arrange quality inspections.

Do you manufacture the products?

No. We are an independent sourcing and procurement service. Products are manufactured by the supplier selected by the buyer.

Do I pay you or the manufacturer?

You pay us separately for the agreed sourcing services. In most projects, you pay the selected manufacturer directly for samples, production, and goods.

Can you guarantee product quality?

No sourcing company can responsibly guarantee that manufacturing defects will never occur. We help reduce risk through clear specifications, supplier verification, approved samples, production follow-ups, and pre-shipment inspections.

Can you find the cheapest supplier?

We can compare pricing and negotiate commercial terms, but we do not recommend choosing a supplier based only on the lowest quotation. Capability, consistency, communication, lead time, and reliability are also important.

How many suppliers will you provide?

The number depends on the project scope. A standard supplier-search project typically includes a focused shortlist of qualified options rather than a large unverified database.

How long does supplier research take?

Timing depends on the product and complexity. A standard sourcing project may take approximately 10-15 business days. Custom manufacturing, certification requirements, and physical verification may require additional time.

Can you arrange samples?

Yes. We can coordinate sample requests, consolidate supplier feedback, communicate revisions, and help compare the samples received. Sample and shipping costs are paid separately.

Can you visit the factory?

Factory visits can be arranged when suitable for the project. Travel, audit, and inspection expenses are agreed before the visit.

Can you help with shipping?

We can coordinate with the buyer, manufacturer, inspection provider, and freight forwarder. Freight, customs clearance, duties, taxes, and shipping contracts are generally managed by the buyer or their appointed logistics provider.

Do you work with startups?

Yes, provided the product, budget, order quantity, and expectations are commercially realistic.

Do you only work with large orders?

No. Suitable order quantities depend on the product and manufacturer. We can search for suppliers that support lower opening quantities, but small orders may have higher unit costs.

Will you reveal the supplier's identity?

Yes. We operate transparently and provide supplier information as agreed within the project scope. Our service is based on professional sourcing support, not withholding factory details.

Do you receive commissions from suppliers?

Any commission arrangement relevant to the project should be disclosed. Our primary responsibility is to the buyer, and commercial terms are agreed transparently before the project begins.

What happens after I submit the form?

We review your requirement and contact you to clarify the product, quantity, budget, specifications, destination, and timeline. We then recommend a project scope and provide a written proposal.
