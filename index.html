How It Works

A Transparent 7-Step Sourcing Workflow

From initial design brief to pre-shipment quality release.

1

Submit Product Specifications

Share your target design, specifications, order quantity, pricing targets, certifications, and target delivery schedule.

2

Market Research & Factory Screening

We leverage our local network in India to identify manufacturers that match your capacity, machinery, and compliance requirements.

3

Receive Structured Comparison Report

Get a comprehensive matrix breaking down unit prices, MOQs, lead times, factory export experience, and potential operational risks.

4

Sample Development & Review

We coordinate physical samples, evaluate materials and tolerances locally, and manage revision feedback with the factory.

5

Final Commercial Negotiation

Finalize production contracts, deposit payment terms, and packaging guidelines with total transparency.

6

On-Site Production Monitoring

We conduct regular factory check-ins, tracking raw material procurement and manufacturing milestones to prevent delays.

7

Pre-Shipment Quality Inspection

Before balance payment is released, we perform final inspections against approved golden samples and send a comprehensive photographic report.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with an India sourcing partner.

What exactly does a sourcing agent in India do?
We act as your local procurement team on the ground. We find verified manufacturers, collect and negotiate quotations, manage sampling, perform factory audits, monitor production progress, and conduct pre-shipment quality inspections.
Do you take hidden commissions from manufacturers?
No. We operate with complete transparency. You pay us directly for our sourcing services. We work exclusively in your interest as a buyer and disclose all pricing directly from the supplier.
How long does a typical supplier search project take?
A standard supplier research project takes 10 to 14 business days. This includes outreach, screening, quotation collection, and compiling a structured comparison report for your review.
Can you conduct pre-shipment quality inspections?
Yes. We perform pre-shipment AQL inspections prior to final balance payments. We check product dimensions, materials, packaging, labeling, and functionality against your approved golden sample.
Do you reveal the manufacturer's identity to us?
Yes! Unlike traditional trading companies that hide factory details, we provide full transparency including factory names, locations, and contact details as part of our deliverables.
